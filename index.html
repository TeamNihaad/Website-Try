THE CHALLENGE

Construction projects generate information.
But insight gets buried.

Contracts, notices, instructions and claim letters tell the story of a project. Yet critical dates, obligations and evidence are scattered across thousands of pages and correspondence threads.

Lexcrete brings that information together — making the project record easier to understand, act on and build a claim from.
